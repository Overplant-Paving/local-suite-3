Latest auction yields by term

The most recent posted high yield for each Treasury security term. Bills are quoted as a coupon-equivalent investment rate; notes and bonds as a high yield. This is what the market actually paid at auction — a close cousin of what you'd earn buying at issue.

 

Average interest rate — now vs. 3 months ago vs. 1 year ago

Treasury's weighted-average interest rate on each class of marketable debt. This is the cost of the whole outstanding stock (slow to move), not today's market yield — useful for seeing the broad direction of rates.

 

The daily par yield curve
