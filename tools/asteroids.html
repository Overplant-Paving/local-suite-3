Near-Earth Asteroid Watch

Close approaches over the next week — how big, how fast, and how close in lunar distances (one LD = the Earth–Moon gap). Perspective with breakfast.

Using the shared NASA demo key (30 requests/hour). Add your own free key →

Free instant key at api.nasa.gov — raises the limit to 1,000/hour. Stored only in this browser (suite.key.nasa). NeoWs caps one request at 7 days, so the 14- and 30-day windows page it (2 and 4 requests per refresh).

Diameter is estimated from absolute magnitude (H) assuming a typical albedo — real sizes vary with reflectivity, so we show a range. Distances and speeds are computed at the moment of closest approach. "Closer than the Moon" (< 1 LD) rows are highlighted.

Data: NASA Near-Earth Object Web Service — NeoWs (api.nasa.gov)
