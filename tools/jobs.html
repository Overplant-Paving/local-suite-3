Update the data

All series are seasonally adjusted. From bls.gov/data grab LNS14000000 (unemployment rate), LNS11300000 (participation rate), and CES0000000001 (total nonfarm employment level, thousands). Paste rows for one series at a time — 2026 M07 4.1 or 2026-07,4.1. Payroll change is computed from the level series. Everything stays on this machine.

Optional: with a personal CORS relay set (see relay/worker.js in the suite repo), fetch the live BLS v1 series through it.

Data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · Current Population Survey & Current Employment Statistics (seasonally adjusted).
